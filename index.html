Our Story

Beauty that Celebrates India

Born from the
Soul of India

Lakmé was established in 1952, when Jawaharlal Nehru urged J.R.D. Tata to start an Indian cosmetics brand — so that Indian women would not spend precious foreign exchange on foreign cosmetics.

The brand is named after the French opera "Lakmé" — derived from the Sanskrit word for Goddess Lakshmi. It has grown into India's most beloved beauty brand, trusted by millions of women across the country.

"People will stare… make it worth their while."

From its humble beginnings to a pan-India phenomenon, Lakmé has always celebrated the vibrant, diverse beauty of Indian women — crafting products made for our skin, our climate, our culture.
